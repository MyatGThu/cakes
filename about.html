The baker behind Aurette

Aurette is not a factory, a franchise, or a “cloud bakery.” It’s Mia, an oven with opinions, and a calendar that only says yes when a cake can be made properly.

Chapter 01 · How it started

It started, like most good things, with a birthday emergency. A friend’s cake order fell through two days before the party, and Mia — armed with her grandmother’s notebook and an optimistic amount of butter — said the four words every baker regrets and treasures: “I can make one.”

The party ate every crumb. Someone asked for a card. There was no card. There was, eventually, an Instagram handle.

Chapter 02 · How it works now

Every Aurette cake is made to order in one Melbourne kitchen — that’s the whole business model. No freezer full of “just in case,” no shelf life to chase. You pick a date, the calendar checks it’s honest, and your cake goes in the oven because it’s yours.

Custom requests are the favourite part. Bring the wild idea. The worst that happens is a conversation about structural engineering and buttercream.
