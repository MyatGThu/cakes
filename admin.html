Quick help

  • Change a price or name: Menu & products → edit the box → Publish changes.
  • Sold out / taking a break: untick “Show on the website” on a product, or set an announcement in Shop settings.
  • New photo: press “Upload photo” on a product. Bright, square-ish photos look best; it's resized automatically.
  • “Needs X days” : that's the product's lead time — the website uses it to work out the earliest pickup date customers can choose.
  • Nothing changed on the site? Publishing takes a minute or two, then refresh the shop page.
