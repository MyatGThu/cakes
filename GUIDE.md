# 🧁 Your website cheat-sheet

Hi! This is the guide for running your cake shop website. No tech skills needed — promise.

## The two links you need

- **Your shop (share this one):** `https://<username>.github.io/cakes/`
- **Your admin page (keep this one for you):** `https://<username>.github.io/cakes/admin.html`

The first time you open the admin page on a phone or computer, paste in the **access key**
you were given. That device stays connected after that.

## The golden rule

Nothing changes on the website until you press **Publish changes** (top-right button).
After you publish, wait a minute or two, then refresh your shop page to see it live.

## Everyday things

**Change a price or description** — Admin → *Menu & products* → edit the box → **Publish changes**.

**Add a new product** — *Menu & products* → **＋ Add a new product** → fill in name, price,
description → set **Days of notice needed** (how many days ahead people must order it) →
**Upload photo** → **Publish changes**.

**Take a photo that sells** 📸 — natural daylight, plain background, cake in the middle.
Landscape (sideways) photos fit the cards best. Upload straight from your phone — the site
shrinks it automatically.

**Something is sold out or you're not making it right now** — untick **Show on the website**
on that product and publish. Tick it again when it's back. (Don't delete it — hiding is easier.)

**Sizes and prices** — tick **This product has sizes / options** to offer e.g. 6" and 8" at
different prices.

**Going on holiday** 🏖️ — *Shop settings* → write an announcement like
*"Taking a break — back baking on the 20th!"* and publish. For a full pause, also untick
**Show on the website** on every product. (Clear the announcement text to remove the banner.)

**Change pickup days or times** — *Shop settings* → tick the days you're closed and edit
the pickup time slots.

**Delivery on or off** — *Shop settings* → tick or untick **Offer delivery**. When it's on,
customers can choose delivery at checkout and their address arrives inside the order message;
you agree the delivery fee with them when you confirm.

## How orders reach you

Customers pick their items and a pickup day (the site automatically only lets them choose
dates that give you enough notice), then send the order to you **on WhatsApp** (or email).
It arrives as a tidy message with the items, sizes, their name, phone number and pickup day.

**You reply to confirm** — that's when you agree the final price for custom work, take any
deposit, and share the pickup address. If a date doesn't suit you, just suggest another in
the chat — nothing is promised until you confirm. 💬

## If something looks stuck

- Changes not showing? Publishing takes a minute or two — refresh the shop page after that.
- Admin page asking for the key again? Paste the same access key you were given.
- Key not working, or anything else weird? Message the person who set the site up. 🙂
